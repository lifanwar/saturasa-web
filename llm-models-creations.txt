# Restaurant Ordering System - Models Context

## Apps Structure:
- core: TenantAwareModel (abstract base)
- menu: Category, MenuItem (multi-currency, stock management)
- customers: Customer (guest/member with phone as unique ID)
- orders: Order, OrderItem, OrderTimeline, CustomerAnalytics

## Key Features:
- Multi-currency: IDR & EGP per menu item
- Multi-channel: ONLINE/OFFLINE × DINE_IN/TAKEAWAY/DELIVERY
- Guest checkout: No registration required (phone + name)
- Member system: Django User integration
- Stock tracking: Manual daily updates
- Analytics: Order timeline & customer stats
- Offline walk-in: No customer record needed

## Business Rules:
- Phone number = unique customer identifier
- Price snapshot in OrderItem
- Auto is_available=False when stock=0
- Guest can upgrade to member (history preserved)
- Status flow: PENDING→CONFIRMED→PREPARING→READY→COMPLETED

## Database Relationships:
- Category 1:N MenuItem
- Customer 1:1 User (nullable)
- Customer 1:N Order (nullable for walk-in)
- Order 1:N OrderItem
- Order 1:N OrderTimeline
- Customer 1:1 CustomerAnalytics
- MenuItem N:1 OrderItem
